## Overview

From 2021 to 2023, I served as a teaching assistant for core quantitative economics courses, including **Econometrics**, **Applied Statistics**, **R and Python Programming**, and **Microeconomics**. 

## Teaching Responsibilities

- Prepared and led tutorials on programming, econometrics, statistics, and microeconomics.
- Developed and reviewed R and Python scripts for course exercises and applied projects.
- Guided students through data analysis workflows and the interpretation of statistical results.
- Graded problem sets and provided clear, constructive feedback on quantitative assignments.
